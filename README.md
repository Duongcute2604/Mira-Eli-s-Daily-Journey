# 🌙 About the Journey

**Mira Eli's Daily Journey** is a story about ordinary days.

There are no monsters to defeat.
No kingdom to save.
No perfect answer waiting at the end.

Just people.

People who wake up late.
People who are tired.
People who love someone but don't know how to say it.
People who have dreams, responsibilities, and sometimes too many things to carry at once.

You play through the life of **Dương** —
trying to balance **family, love, work, study, and herself**.

Some choices change the story.

Some choices only change a single conversation.

And some choices may seem meaningless...

until much later.

---

## 🕰️ Every Day Leaves Something Behind

The game remembers.

A conversation you avoided.
A message you answered.
A hand you accepted.
A person you chose to stay with.
A dream you decided not to abandon.

Not every decision leads to a dramatic consequence.

Sometimes the biggest change is simply:

> **who you become after making it.**

---

## ❤️ The Three People at the Center

### Dương
The person trying to keep everything together.

### Nam
The person who makes Dương question what she really wants.

### Minh
The person who reminds Dương that loving someone
doesn't always mean deciding their future for them.

---

## 🌱 There Is No Perfect Ending

The journey can lead to different futures.

Some are happy.

Some are bittersweet.

Some are painful.

Some are simply... honest.

The goal isn't to find the "correct" ending.

The goal is to find the ending that feels like **your journey**.
